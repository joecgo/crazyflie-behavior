# Backlog #70: The laptop stalls, and the stale-data failsafe landed the hover

The row's history, moved from the [decision backlog](../decision-backlog.md) on 2026-11-16. The row keeps the open question.

**The laptop stalls every 15 s, and the stale-data failsafe landed the hover.** Tick overruns and telemetry gaps of up to 0.34 s, every 15.0 s on the same wall-clock seconds, also in mock runs with no drone. Session 1 didn't show them. Find the cause first (another program, the power plan; compare runs from a plain terminal with the Claude app closed), then decide whether the 0.25 s stale limit needs a margin for it. A 65 s timing probe on 2026-11-08 (plain Python, no project code) showed no 15 s stalls, so the cause was present on 2026-11-07 and not on 11-08. A Windows laptop is never real-time, so the loop can't be made immune; only the response to a late tick can be designed. **Trying first (Joe, 2026-11-08): A. Make stalls rarer:** higher process priority, Windows power throttling off for the process, on the charger with a high-performance power plan, and LatencyMon to find driver stalls (unchecked hunch: the battery driver's periodic check on some laptops). This reduces stalls but can't remove them, and priority doesn't help against driver stalls. **Still open:** (B) the safety layer tells a laptop stall from lost data: after a late tick, take in the backlog and judge age by the drone's timestamps; (C) set the longest laptop stall the safety layer accepts, per mode, from what the drone does on its own (the hover and the take-off climb hold position on board; in steering mode the setpoint timeout stops following at 0.5 s and cuts the motors at 2 s, [ADR-0011](../decisions/0011-mock-models-setpoint-timeout.md)); (D) move time-critical parts onto the drone (see #12). AI recommendation: B with C, plus A. **Session 4 (Joe, 2026-11-12): A only**, on the charger with a high-performance power plan; the Claude app stays open. No overruns on 2026-11-08 or 11-09 (worst lateness 17 ms); 11-07 had 1-4 per flight, up to 189 ms. Each test log records the tick loop summary.
