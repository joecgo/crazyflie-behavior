# Catch the bird

The rules of the game ([ADR-0015](decisions/0015-first-game-catch-the-bird.md)).

> **Draft.** The numbers here (round length, wait times, speeds) are starting
> points, to be tuned in playtests (see the decision backlog).

## The idea

The drone is a bird. It flies around the room, always above the player's reach,
and stops to rest. The player chases it, and when they get right underneath it,
that's a **catch**: the bird wiggles and chirps, then flies off to a new spot,
a little faster each time.

## Setting up

- **A clear play area:** nothing on the floor to trip over, away from couches,
  tables and anything a child could climb.
- **Ceiling fans off.**
- **The bird's nest:** a landing pad in a corner of the play area, where the
  bird takes off and lands.
- **A grown-up in charge,** holding the kill switch for the whole game.

## How to play

1. **Start:** the grown-up starts a round. The bird takes off from its nest and
   rises into the **sky layer**, high above the player's reach.
2. **The bird rests:** it flies to a spot, hovers there and sings (sounds from
   the computer) so the player knows where it is.
3. **Catch!** The player runs underneath the bird. The bird sees them below,
   wiggles, chirps happily, and scores a point.
4. **Fly away:** the bird flies to a new resting spot. After each catch it
   rests a little less and flies a little farther.
5. **End of the round:** after about a minute, the bird does a celebration
   flight, announces the score, and flies home to its nest to land.

## Difficulty

- **It gets harder** with each catch: shorter rests, longer and faster flights.
- **It also helps a player who's struggling** (dynamic difficulty): if a catch
  takes a long time, the bird slows down and rests longer, so the game stays
  fun and the player wins often.

## The bird's moods

| Mood | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Resting** | Hovers gently with a little bob and sings |
| **Caught** | A happy wiggle and chirp |
| **Flying off** | Darts to its next spot |
| **Tired** (low battery, or the round is over) | A sleepy sound, then flies home to its nest and lands |

## Safety rules

The game is designed so the bird never comes down to the player. These rules
keep it that way:

- **The bird always stays above reach.** It keeps about a meter of clearance
  below it at all times; if anything rises toward it, it moves away sideways
  (the **clearance rule**).
- **No climbing** on furniture during the game.
- **Grown-ups sit on the floor** while the bird is flying: the sky layer is at
  an adult's head height.
- **No throwing toys** at the bird.
- **Propeller guards on.**
- **The kill switch is always in a grown-up's hand.** If anything looks wrong,
  stop the motors.
- **Tested with stand-ins first:** every new version of the bird is tried with
  an adult and a raised broom standing in for the player, before any child plays.
