| 19 | **Parked ([ADR-0015](decisions/0015-first-game-catch-the-bird.md)).** **Player tracking with UWB radio tags (Bitcraze Loco): the chosen direction.** The player wears a small UWB node (e.g. on a hat; the head is also the best spot for the signal, since bodies block UWB), and the drone measures where the player is to about 10 cm, in any direction, including behind it. That gives drone-to-player distance, not direction: direction would come from moving and re-measuring, or from the range sensors. **Still to check before buying:** (2) full positioning with anchors (setup per room) or drone-to-player distance only (no anchors), and whether cflib exposes the distance-only mode; (3) answered (Bitcraze compatibility matrix): Flow, Multi-ranger and Loco are all compatible with standard firmware. Flow underneath, Multi-ranger on top, Loco on either side; three decks need a longer pin-header set. | 26 | **A hung tick drops the drone** (found writing [ADR-0024](decisions/0024-tick-timing-strategy.md)). The tick loop can't interrupt a tick that hangs, so setpoints stop.
