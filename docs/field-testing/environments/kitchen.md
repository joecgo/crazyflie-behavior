# Environment: kitchen

A static room: nothing moves while the drone flies. Test logs and session docs
link here and name a location and a lighting setup; a test log adds any extra
description (time of day, sunlight) itself.

A semi-open layout joins it to the [living room](living-room.md), so some light
spreads between the two rooms (Joe, 2026-11-12).

Used for the Hover in
[First flight 1](../sessions/2026-11-04-first-flight-1.md) and for every test in
[First flight 4](../sessions/2026-11-12-first-flight-4.md), at the center of the room.

## Daylight and windows

An east-facing sliding glass door and a smaller south-facing window over the
sink (Joe, 2026-11-12).

## Obstacles and reflective surfaces

*Anything the range sensors or Flow deck may struggle with: glass, mirrors, dark surfaces.*

- Glass: the east-facing sliding glass door, the south-facing window over the sink, and the glass panes in a small cabinet's doors (Joe, 2026-11-12)
- Dark surfaces: a black range and range hood (the oven door has a glass window), and a black dishwasher (Joe, 2026-11-12)
- Shiny metal: a steel sink and a steel-colored fridge (Joe, 2026-11-12)
